# Attribution and provenance

## Prior mathematical work

The density-limit theorem and finite-prime series framework appear in the
September 26, 2026 companion manuscript of the Continual Graph Memory project.
The proof here develops that framework, including its sieve argument, with a
uniform quantitative removal estimate and explicit logarithmic rate. The earlier
work receives the mathematical credit for the density-limit framework.
[September manuscript](https://github.com/uclanlp/continual-graph-memory/blob/23367c6568c344d9c4dbf59be3f39b39f5d54324/results/erdos_302.md)
[ArXiv version 1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02945v1)

Stijn Cambie's construction gives the lower bound 5/8 and the negative answer
to the proposed value 1/2. The construction is recorded with the original
problem. Della Pietra's work supplies relevant regularity ideas, as acknowledged
in the paper's bibliography. The original problem is due to Erdős and Graham.
[Problem 302](https://www.erdosproblems.com/302)
[Della Pietra manuscript](https://github.com/donalddellapietra/erdos-301-proof/blob/789c6f045dbc81da3811031247d186a7128dafce/paper/main.tex)
